Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KB Content Manager ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you connect and authenticate using third-party social media platforms, including TikTok and Meta (Instagram & Facebook).

1. Information We Collect

When you authorize KB Content Manager via OAuth, we only access information required to perform content publishing services:

  • Account Identity: User ID, username, and basic public profile information.
  • Authorization Tokens: Secure OAuth access and refresh tokens to schedule and post videos on your behalf.

We do not collect passwords, private messages, or personal payment data.

2. How We Use Information

The information collected is used solely to:

  • Authenticate authorized account managers.
  • Check video posting eligibility (such as maximum duration and allowed privacy levels via official APIs).
  • Publish scheduled video content directly to your authorized social media channels.

3. Data Storage and Security

All authentication tokens are transmitted via encrypted HTTPS and stored securely. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with unauthorized third parties.

4. Data Deletion and Revocation

Users may revoke the App's access at any time through their TikTok or Meta account settings. Upon access revocation, all associated access tokens are immediately invalidated.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@kingbalance.com
